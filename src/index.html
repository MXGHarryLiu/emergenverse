Key And Mouse Controls

6-DOF camera control (when camera lock is off)

  • Translate (keyboard/mouse): `A/S/D/Q/W/E` for camera-relative XYZ movement, or hold right mouse and drag.
  • Translate (touch): Two-finger drag moves the camera relative to the current view. In perspective it strafes and moves forward/back like `A/D` and `W/S`; in top orthographic it pans across the plane.
  • Yaw (keyboard/mouse): `ArrowLeft` / `ArrowRight`, or left-drag horizontally (perspective mode).
  • Pitch (keyboard/mouse): `ArrowUp` / `ArrowDown`, or left-drag vertically (perspective mode).
  • Roll (keyboard): `[` / `]`.
  • Boost (keyboard): Hold `Shift` while translating.
  • Zoom / View Scale (mouse/keyboard/touch): Scroll wheel, pinch with two fingers, or `<` / `>` (same keys as `,` / `.`) changes FOV in perspective and zoom level in top orthographic.
  • Simulation controls: Use the Simulation, Visual, Camera, and World panels to adjust model and rendering parameters.
